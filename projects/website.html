Overview

A shorter project, still fully designed

This project focused on the UX and UI of the Xure marketing website — the pages people land on before they ever open the app. The scope was narrower than Xtore or Vault, covering four page types, but the goal was the same: make the content easier to find and understand.

The designs here were completed but not shipped, so this case study is presented as a concept — not as a live redesign with real usage behind it.

Context

Four pages, four different jobs

The site needed to cover fairly different kinds of content under one consistent system: a company-facing About page, an evolving library of Articles, a reference-style FAQ, and a conversion-oriented Sell on Xure page aimed at prospective merchants.

AboutArticlesHelp CenterSell on Xure

Challenge

Making unrelated content feel like one site

About, Articles, Help Center, and Sell on Xure each have a different purpose and a different amount of content, which made it easy for the site to feel like four disconnected pages. The challenge was building a shared page structure and navigation system flexible enough to hold all four without forcing any of them into a layout that didn't fit their content.

Information Architecture

Structuring the site before the pages

Before designing individual pages, I mapped how each section's content should be organized internally — what belonged on a landing page versus a detail view, and how someone would move between them.

Final Concept

A consistent shell, four distinct pages

The final concept used one shared navigation and typographic system across all four page types, with each page's internal layout shaped by its own content rather than a single forced template.

Reflection

What I learned

01

A shared system doesn't mean every page should look the same.

02

Information architecture decisions come before any visual design.

03

Not every completed design gets shipped. That doesn't make the work less real.

04

Smaller-scope projects still deserve a clear, considered structure.
